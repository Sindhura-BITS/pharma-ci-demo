04 / HYPOTHETICAL PRODUCT X

Product X Decision Simulator

Product X represents a possible new Type 2 diabetes medicine that has not yet generated observed Medicare utilization in this research. Instead of inventing sales or claims, we test how attractive it could be under three benchmark-based commercial conditions.

01 · WHY HYPOTHETICAL?No observed Product X CMS history

There is no measured product utilization to score. The simulator uses existing medicines as reference points—not Product X forecasts.

02 · HOW SCORES ARE BUILTSame WSM method, different assumptions

For each case, benchmark values are normalized on the existing five-drug scale and multiplied by the fixed final weights.

03 · WHAT DECISION IT SUPPORTSAssess pre-launch commercial potential

Compare poor, moderate and optimistic conditions to understand uncertainty before considering major investment.

Try changing the Product X assumptions

Hypothetical only
What stays the same in every scenario: utilization is anchored to the lowest existing benchmark (1,307,099), not Product X claims; disease-burden score is 4/5. The trial count is only a competition proxy: fewer trials score more favorably under this model.
HOW PRODUCT X'S WSM SCORE IS CALCULATED
WSM = Σ (normalized score × weight) = 0.233

For each indicator, convert the scenario assumption to a 0–1 score, multiply by its final weight, then add the seven contributions.
